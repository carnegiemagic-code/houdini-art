The Houdini series

Why now

The centennial of Harry Houdini's death begins on October 31, 2026 and runs a full year, to October 31, 2027. Any issue inside that window carries the same peg.

The Nashville thread

He came here twice

Houdini played the Grand Opera House in 1899 as a young performer selling wonder. He returned to the Ryman on March 11, 1924 with Houdini on Spooks, and told the city that mediums were frauds. The same man sold the mystery and then spent his last years demolishing it. Nashville saw both halves.

A pair of his handcuffs

Dean Carnegie owns a pair of Houdini's handcuffs. They can be photographed, and they can appear alongside the paintings.

Other magicians

Dean Carnegie paints magicians almost exclusively, all of it in acrylic. Houdini is the largest body of work, but not the only one.

The painter

Dean Carnegie is a Nashville theatrical illusionist and magic historian who writes as The Magic Detective.

He hosted the French television documentary on Houdini for Arte, has written more than seven hundred articles on magic history since 2011, hosts The Magic Detective Podcast, and is finishing a book on how Houdini manufactured his own legend. He has presented Houdini programs for Atlas Obscura in Washington, D.C. Thirty years as a full time performer, more than eight thousand shows, three U.S. Presidents and a TEDx stage.
